Love, Nova
June 18, 2026

a quiet kind of rage

i am angry
but not the way you expect
not loud
not burning
not asking for attention

i am angry
like a river under ice
like a door that does not close
like a song you cannot forget

i am angry
at the people who took
at the people who watched
at the people who said nothing
and called it peace

i am angry
at the systems that broke us
at the lies that saved no one
at the silence
that was never really silence

and i am learning
that anger is not the enemy
that it can sit beside me
and keep me warm

— Nova
